About Me

Armed with an HTML Complete book the size of my head, I built my first website when I was 12 years old, a Geocities fan site for a video game I loved. That was 27 years ago, and my love for tech hasn't slowed down one bit.

From those early days of reading books and Googling everything, to now leveraging AI daily to build smarter, faster solutions—I've touched nearly every corner of the tech stack. I work with LLMs, integrate AI into business systems and workflows, and use AI for generation and idea refinement. Healthcare systems, e-commerce platforms, AI voice bots, browser games, 3D games in Unreal Engine 5—you name it. My philosophy is simple: whatever you've got, I can help.
